I am Junteng Liu, a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), advised by Professor Junxian He. My research focuses on Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Machine Learning.

======
* LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
* Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
* LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
